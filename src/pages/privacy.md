# Privacy Policy

> **The full text of this Privacy Policy prevails over this summary.**
>
> - We are Untitled Build Private Limited, an Indian software studio. This policy covers our website, our client services, and our products.
> - We collect what you give us directly (contact form, job applications), a small amount automatically (analytics, log data), and nothing from data brokers.

## 1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Untitled Build Private Limited, operating as **untitled build** ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you:

- visit or use our website at [untitledbuild.com](https://untitledbuild.com) (the "**Site**");
- apply for a role with us through our careers page;
- engage us as a client for design, engineering, AI, cloud, or security services (our "**Services**"); or
- use any current or future software product we build and operate (our "**Products**") — to be updated as products launch.

This policy does **not** govern personal data that we process on a client's behalf, as their processor, inside a client engagement. See [Section 14](#14-client-project-data).

By using the Site, applying for a role, or using a Product, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is the legal basis for processing (for example, non-essential cookies or AI training), we will ask for it separately and you may withdraw it at any time.

## 5. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use the following categories of cookies and similar technologies on the Site. You can manage your preferences at any time through the **Usercentrics consent banner** that appears when you first visit the Site, or by clearing your browser's cookies.

The Analytics category (Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager) is not loaded at all until you grant consent through that banner - not merely hidden or paused, but not requested from Google's servers in the first place.

| Category | Purpose | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Remembering your cookie consent choices | Usercentrics |
| Analytics | Understanding how visitors use the Site, in aggregate | Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager |
| Functional | Powering the live chat widget | Brevo Conversations |

Google Analytics 4 typically sets cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_<container-ID>`, generally retained for up to 13-24 months, to distinguish visitors. Because Usercentrics can add or reconfigure cookies over time, the current, definitive list is always available inside the consent banner itself (open it and select "Details" / "Show purposes") rather than reproduced here, where it would go stale.

**How to opt out:**

- Adjust your preferences at any time via the Usercentrics banner (available on every page of the Site).
- Use [Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to opt out of Google Analytics specifically.
- Configure your browser to block or delete cookies; note that some Site functionality (like the contact form's spam protection) may not work correctly if you do.

## 6. AI and automated processing

**We do not use personal data submitted through this Site, or personal data belonging to our Product users, to train or fine-tune AI/ML models, unless we have your explicit, informed, written consent to do so.**

Where our Services or Products use AI systems (for example, models provided by third-party AI vendors), we or our client may use such systems to process data as part of delivering a feature or engagement, subject to the applicable client agreement. As of this policy's drafting, no third-party AI vendor processes personal data collected through this Site in its own right. Where a specific engagement or Product does route personal data through an AI vendor, that vendor will be named in the applicable SOW or in product-specific terms.

**No solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.** We do not use your personal data to make decisions about you (such as hiring decisions) based solely on automated processing, without meaningful human involvement. A human reviews every job application and every contact form enquiry we act on.

## 8. International data transfers

We are based in India. Some of our sub-processors (for example, Google and Usercentrics) may process data outside India, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or the United States.

- **From the EEA/UK:** where we or our sub-processors transfer personal data outside the EEA/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum, as implemented by the relevant sub-processor.
- **To/from India:** the DPDP Act, 2023 permits transfer of personal data outside India except to countries restricted by the Central Government. We will comply with any such restrictions as they are notified.
- Our infrastructure providers, including our database and file-storage provider (Supabase), may process and store data outside India. Where they do, we require safeguards consistent with the DPDP Act, 2023 and, where applicable, the GDPR/UK GDPR's international-transfer requirements.

## 10. Security measures

We take reasonable, industry-appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS across the Site and our hosting);
- access controls restricting who inside our team can access personal data, including Row-Level Security policies on our database that limit what our public website can do to *insert-only*, so form submissions cannot be read back through the Site itself;
- storing uploaded résumés in a private file storage bucket that is not publicly accessible;
- confidentiality obligations on everyone with access to personal data as part of their role.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will notify affected individuals and, where legally required, regulators, in the event of a data breach likely to result in harm, as described in [Section 13](#13-data-breach-notification).

## 11. Your rights

Your rights depend on where you are located.

### If you are in India (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

- obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities;
- request correction, completion, or updating of your personal data;
- request erasure of your personal data, once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
- nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity;
- withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it;
- file a complaint with us in the first instance, and escalate to the **Data Protection Board of India** if unsatisfied with our response.

### If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR/UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

### If you are in California or another US state (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws)

You have the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccurate personal data, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data. **We do not sell personal data**, and we do not knowingly "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined under the CPRA. If this changes, we will add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism to this Site before doing so.

### How to exercise these rights

Contact us at `hello@untitledbuild.com` or write to our Grievance Officer (Section 2). We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and, where permitted by applicable law, may charge a reasonable fee for, or decline, requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive.

## 12. Children's data

Our Site, Services, and Products are not directed at children. The minimum age to use our Products is 18. Where the DPDP Act, 2023 requires verifiable parental or guardian consent for processing a child's personal data, we will obtain it before processing. If we learn we have collected personal data from a child without appropriate consent (including under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, for US users), we will delete it.

## 13. Data breach notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to cause harm to affected individuals, we will:

- take immediate steps to contain and assess the breach;
- notify the **Data Protection Board of India** and affected Data Principals as required under the DPDP Act, 2023;
- notify the relevant EU/UK supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies and the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, and notify affected individuals where the risk is high;
- notify affected US residents as required by applicable state breach notification laws.

## 14. Client project data

When we deliver Services to a client, we frequently process personal data (for example, the client's own customer or employee data) strictly on that client's documented instructions, for the purpose of building, testing, or operating the software we've been engaged to build.

In that relationship:

- **the client is the data controller / Data Fiduciary**, and we act as their **data processor / Significant Data Fiduciary's processor**, as applicable;
- this Privacy Policy does not govern that processing;
- the terms of that processing (including sub-processors we may use, security commitments, and data deletion on termination) are set out in a separate **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**, incorporated into the applicable MSA/SOW with that client.

If you believe your personal data has been processed by us on behalf of one of our clients and you wish to exercise a data-subject right, please contact that client directly, or contact us and we will direct your request appropriately.

## 16. Contact and grievance redressal

If you have a question, concern, or complaint about how we handle personal data:

1. **Contact us first**, at `hello@untitledbuild.com` or through our [Grievance Officer](#2-who-we-are-and-how-to-contact-us). We aim to acknowledge grievances within a reasonable period and resolve them within the timeline prescribed under applicable law.
2. **If you're in India and unsatisfied with our response**, you may escalate your complaint to the **Data Protection Board of India**, established under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
3. **If you're in the EEA/UK**, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
4. **If you're in the US**, you may contact your state Attorney General's office regarding rights under applicable state privacy law.
