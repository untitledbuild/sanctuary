# Privacy Policy

> **The full text of this Privacy Policy prevails over this summary.**
>
> - We are Untitled Build Private Limited, an Indian software studio. This policy covers our website, our client services, and our products.
> - We collect what you give us directly (contact form, job applications), a small amount automatically (analytics, log data), and nothing from data brokers.

## 11. Your rights

Your rights depend on where you are located.

### If you are in India (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

- obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities;
- request correction, completion, or updating of your personal data;
- request erasure of your personal data, once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
- nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity;
- withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it;
- file a complaint with us in the first instance, and escalate to the **Data Protection Board of India** if unsatisfied with our response.

### If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR/UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

### If you are in California or another US state (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws)

You have the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccurate personal data, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data. **We do not sell personal data**, and we do not knowingly "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined under the CPRA. `[PLACEHOLDER: if this changes, add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism here]`

## 13. Data breach notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to cause harm to affected individuals, we will:

- take immediate steps to contain and assess the breach;
- notify the **Data Protection Board of India** and affected Data Principals as required under the DPDP Act, 2023;
- notify the relevant EU/UK supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies and the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, and notify affected individuals where the risk is high;
- notify affected US residents as required by applicable state breach notification laws.

## 14. Client project data

When we deliver Services to a client, we frequently process personal data (for example, the client's own customer or employee data) strictly on that client's documented instructions, for the purpose of building, testing, or operating the software we've been engaged to build.

In that relationship:

- **the client is the data controller / Data Fiduciary**, and we act as their **data processor / Significant Data Fiduciary's processor**, as applicable;
- this Privacy Policy does not govern that processing;
- the terms of that processing (including sub-processors we may use, security commitments, and data deletion on termination) are set out in a separate **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**, incorporated into the applicable MSA/SOW with that client.

If you believe your personal data has been processed by us on behalf of one of our clients and you wish to exercise a data-subject right, please contact that client directly, or contact us and we will direct your request appropriately.
